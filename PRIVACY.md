The short version
Saints Companion does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It works without an internet connection.

What the app stores on your device
Saints Companion saves two settings only on your device, so the app remembers your choices:

the saints you mark as favorites, and
the language you choose (English or Spanish).
This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it.

What the app does not do
It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.
It does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or any form of tracking.
It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or social-media code.
It does not make network requests. All saints, images, and prayers are included in the app.
It does not ask for any device permissions.
Links and sharing
The Image credits screen has links to the source pages of the portraits on Wikimedia Commons. If you tap one, it opens in your web browser. That website is run by others and has its own privacy policy.
The Share buttons use the standard iOS share sheet. What you share, and with whom, is your choice; the app does not see or store it.
Information handled by Apple
If you have allowed Apple to share analytics with developers in your device settings, Apple may give us aggregate, anonymous statistics about the app (for example, download counts or crash reports). This is collected and governed by Apple, not by Saints Companion, and it does not include anything that identifies you. See Apple's privacy policy for details.

Children
The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices and rights
Because we do not collect or hold any personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete. You can remove everything the app has stored on your device at any time by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top. If a future version of the app ever collects data, this policy will be updated first and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
